The Cryptography of Legal Digital Signatures: eIDAS, ESIGN, & SHA-256 Audit Trails

A technical examination of how EasyWorkFlow™ enforces tamper-evidence, PKI asymmetric key pairs, and legally unassailable cryptographic certificates of completion.

1. Digital Signatures vs. Electronic Signatures

While the term "electronic signature" can refer simply to a scanned image of handwriting or an email confirmation, a true cryptographic digital signature relies on asymmetric Public Key Infrastructure (PKI).

Under both the United States ESIGN Act (15 U.S.C. ch. 96) and European Union eIDAS Regulation (No 910/2014), legal enforceability requires establishing three non-negotiable guarantees:

  1. Signer Authentication: Proof of identity and intent at the exact moment of execution.
  2. Document Integrity: Proof that not a single byte, comma, or numeral was altered after signing.
  3. Non-Repudiation: Prevention of any signatory denying their execution due to unassailable forensic evidence.
crypto_envelope_seal.js // PKI Sealing Sequence 
import crypto from 'node:crypto';

// 1. Calculate Canonical SHA-256 Digest of Executed PDF
const documentHash = crypto
  .createHash('sha256')
  .update(pdfBuffer)
  .digest('hex');

// 2. Sign with HSM Private Key (ECDSA P-256 / secp256r1)
const signer = crypto.createSign('SHA256');
signer.update(JSON.stringify({ documentHash, timestamp: Date.now(), ipGeolocation }));
const digitalSignature = signer.sign(hsmPrivateKey, 'hex');

// 3. Issue RFC 3161 Timestamped Completion Certificate
const certOfCompletion = await issueTsaStamp(digitalSignature);

3. RFC 3161 Trusted Timestamp Authorities (TSA)

Local server clocks can drift or be spoofed. EasyWorkFlow integrates with globally trusted RFC 3161 Timestamping Authorities (TSA) to bind the document hash to an atomic clock time-stamp cryptographically signed by an independent certified third party.

4. Court Admissibility & Audit Trail Forensics

Enterprise trust requires mathematical rigor. By pairing asymmetric ECDSA keys, RFC 3161 timestamps, and immutable audit trails, EasyWorkFlow™ guarantees that cross-border agreements executed through our platform stand up to the most demanding international court challenges.
