Commercial & Billing Transparency

Refund & Cancellation Policy

Effective Date: August 17, 2026 • Version 2.2 • Multi-Gateway Commercial Terms

At Easy Labs Ltd ("Easy Labs", "we", "our", or "us"), we stand firmly behind the engineering quality, reliability, and security of our software platforms and consulting services. This Refund & Cancellation Policy defines the transparent rules governing refunds, subscription cancellations, and settlement channels across all our software offerings (EasySchoolFlow™, EasyWorkFlow™, EsERP™, CareflowHMS™) and custom technology deliverables processed globally and regionally through licensed payment gateways including Flutterwave, Paystack, and Stripe.

1. SaaS Subscriptions & 14-Day Money-Back Guarantee

We want all organizations to experience the speed, workflow automation, and security of our platforms with complete peace of mind:

First-Time Annual Subscription Guarantee:

For new enterprise clients purchasing an annual subscription to any of our cloud-hosted SaaS solutions (including EasySchoolFlow™, EasyWorkFlow™, EsERP™, or CareflowHMS™), we offer an unconditional 14-day money-back guarantee from the initial billing date. If the platform does not satisfy your operational requirements within the first 14 calendar days, you may request a full 100% refund of your subscription fee.

Monthly Subscriptions: Monthly subscription tiers are billed on a recurring 30-day basis. You may cancel your subscription at any time via your organization's administrative dashboard or by contacting support. Upon cancellation, your access will remain fully functional until the end of the current billing cycle, and no further recurring charges will occur. Pre-paid monthly periods that have already commenced are non-refundable.

Renewals & Advance Notice: Annual subscriptions will automatically notify primary account administrators via email at least 30 calendar days prior to renewal. Once an annual subscription renews, cancellation requests submitted within 7 business days of the renewal date will receive a pro-rated refund minus any transaction processing charges.

2. Enterprise Sovereign Cloud & On-Premise Deployments

Due to the specialized infrastructure orchestration, customized database architecture, and private container provisioning required for Custom Sovereign Cloud (AWS/GCP/Azure) and air-gapped On-Premises installations:

  • One-Time Deployment & Setup Fees: Engineering fees associated with infrastructure provisioning, VPC peering, data migration, and custom single sign-on (SSO) integration are non-refundable once engineering deployment has commenced.
  • Enterprise Annual Software Licenses: Software license fees for sovereign deployments are refundable during the initial 30-day acceptance testing period if the software fails to meet verified technical specifications agreed upon in the signed Order Form, provided Easy Labs is afforded a 14-day cure window to remediate any deficiencies.

3. Custom Engineering Services & Statement of Work (SOW)

For bespoke software development, AI model integration, and custom enterprise workflows delivered under an executed Statement of Work (SOW):

  • Milestone-Based Billing: Work is delivered in structured milestones with formal client sign-off criteria. Once a milestone has been reviewed, approved, or accepted by the client in writing, the corresponding milestone payment is final and non-refundable.
  • Unstarted Milestones: If an engagement is terminated early by either party in accordance with the Master Services Agreement, any unearned advance retainer or deposit allocated to milestones that have not yet commenced will be refunded pro-rata, deducting documented hours and pass-through costs already incurred.
  • Discovery Sprints: Initial architectural discovery, technical feasibility sprints, and wireframing phases are non-refundable once deliverables have been transmitted to the client.

5. Payment Processors & Gateway Settlement Terms

Easy Labs Ltd utilizes internationally regulated payment gateways to provide seamless, secure billing across global and regional markets. All refunds are credited directly back to the original funding source through the processor utilized during the initial transaction:

Paystack

Cards • Transfers • USSD

Primary gateway for enterprise and SaaS transactions in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, and South Africa (NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR, USD).

  • Card Refunds: Reversal directly to the customer's debit/credit card within 3–7 business days depending on the issuing bank's clearing cycle.
  • Bank Transfers & USSD: Credited back to the originating bank account within 1–3 business days via Paystack automated reversal rails.

Flutterwave

Mobile Money • Multi-Currency

Multi-currency processing across African and global corridors supporting cards, bank accounts, and mobile wallets.

  • Mobile Money (M-Pesa, MTN MoMo, Airtel, Vodafone): Settled directly back into the originating mobile wallet within 24–48 hours.
  • Cards & Account Debits: Reversal processed within 3–7 business days based on local switch and interbank clearing times.

Stripe

Global Cards • SEPA • ACH

International payment infrastructure for enterprise clients in North America, Europe, and worldwide (USD, GBP, EUR).

  • Credit / Debit Cards: Settled to original card within 5–10 business days depending on card network clearing houses.
  • SEPA / ACH / Wire: Returned to original bank account within 3–7 business days after initiation.

Multi-Currency Invoicing & Foreign Exchange: Refunds are disbursed in the exact denomination/currency in which the customer was originally billed. Easy Labs is not liable for variances resulting from daily foreign exchange rate fluctuations or bank-imposed international conversion charges between the original transaction timestamp and the refund settlement date.

Payment Gateway Transaction Fees: Under standard rules enforced by Flutterwave, Paystack, and Stripe, gateway interchange and network processing fees incurred at the time of purchase are retained by payment networks and non-refundable, except in verified instances of platform billing duplication or technical errors attributable to Easy Labs.

6. Non-Refundable Items

Except where mandated by applicable consumer protection laws, the following items are strictly non-refundable:

  • Third-party pass-through costs (such as dedicated Twilio SMS credits, Brevo email volume allocations, non-refundable Flutterwave, Paystack, or Stripe gateway transaction charges, or custom domain registration fees).
  • Accounts terminated due to violations of our Acceptable Use Policy, fraudulent activity, or intentional compromise of platform security.
  • Custom source code repositories transferred and hosted within client-owned private repositories following SOW project sign-off.

7. Refund Request Procedure

To initiate an official refund or billing review request, please follow these simple steps:

  1. Send an email directly to billing@easylabsltd.com with a copy to info@easylabsltd.com.
  2. Use the subject line: [Refund Request] - Organization Name - Invoice #.
  3. Include in the body: Your organization account ID, registered administrator email, date of payment, reason for request, and the corresponding payment gateway reference:
    • Paystack: Paystack Transaction Reference (e.g., pstk_... or reference number)
    • Flutterwave: Flutterwave Transaction ID or Reference (e.g., flw_ref_...)
    • Stripe: Stripe Charge ID or Receipt Number (e.g., ch_... / pi_...)
    • Direct Wire / Bank Transfer: Bank payment reference or wire advice
Processing & Settlement Timeline:

Our finance team will evaluate your request within 3 to 5 business days. Once approved, payout turnaround depends on your original payment channel:
• Mobile Money Wallets (Flutterwave): 24 to 48 hours.
• Local Bank Cards & Accounts (Paystack & Flutterwave): 3 to 7 business days.
• International Cards & Bank Wire (Stripe): 5 to 10 business days.

8. Chargeback & Dispute Protocol

We strongly urge all enterprise clients and institution administrators to contact our commercial team prior to lodging an official dispute or chargeback with your bank, card network, or mobile money provider (via Flutterwave Chargeback Management, Paystack Dispute Center, or Stripe Radar). Initiating formal chargebacks without prior communication automatically flags the account, which may trigger automated suspension of API endpoints, webhooks, and sub-accounts pending regulatory resolution. We are committed to transparent, amicable billing resolution and will swiftly investigate and correct any billing discrepancies.
