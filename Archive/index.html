Frequently Asked Questions

Everything You Need to Know About Easy Labs

Clear answers regarding our technology consulting engagements, flagship software products, and security protocols.

What services does Easy Labs Ltd provide?
Easy Labs Ltd is a technology consulting and software engineering firm. We design, architect, and deploy AI-native platforms, enterprise web and mobile applications, custom ERP software, EdTech platforms (EasyLearn), and Hospital Management Information Systems (EsMed / Nexiora).
Can EasyLearn be customized for our university or school network?
Yes. EasyLearn is built on a modular, API-first architecture. It integrates with your existing identity providers (SSO / OAuth / Active Directory), campus payment gateways, biometric attendance hardware, and custom academic grading frameworks.
How does EsMed (Nexiora) handle medical data privacy and compliance?
Nexiora conforms strictly to HIPAA, GDPR, and HL7/FHIR health data standards. All patient Electronic Health Records (EHR) and clinical notes are encrypted both in transit (TLS 1.3) and at rest (AES-256) with granular role-based access control (RBAC) and tamper-proof audit trails.
What is your typical project delivery and development timeline?
Our agile sprints allow MVP prototypes to be validated in 4–6 weeks. Production-grade enterprise platforms and custom ERP integrations typically ship in 8–16 weeks with continuous CI/CD automated staging deployments.
How do we get started on an engagement with Easy Labs?
You can submit a project brief through our consultation form below or schedule an architecture scoping call. Our Solutions Architects will review your technical requirements and prepare a detailed blueprint and timeline proposal within 24 hours.
