Security & Trust Center

Enterprise Security, HIPAA Compliance & Trust Architecture

CISO Security Baseline • Version 2026.3 • Zero-Trust Protocol

Security at Easy Labs Ltd is foundational, not an afterthought. We engineer all products—EasySchoolFlow™, EasyWorkFlow™, EsERP™, and CareflowHMS™—to the highest global cryptographic and clinical compliance standards.

1. Infrastructure & Encryption Standards

🔒 In-Flight Encryption

Enforced TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy across all microservices, API gateways, and client connections.

🛡️ At-Rest Encryption

FIPS 140-2 validated AES-256-GCM envelope encryption with AWS/GCP Key Management Service (KMS) or customer-managed keys (BYOK).

2. Health Insurance Portability and Accountability Act (HIPAA) & BAA

For healthcare organizations utilizing CareflowHMS™ (Hospital Management System):

  • Business Associate Agreement (BAA): Easy Labs executes standard BAAs with covered entities guaranteeing PHI (Protected Health Information) confidentiality.
  • Field-Level PHI Encryption: Medical diagnostics, patient identities, and clinical charting are encrypted before persistence.
  • Audit Logs: Immutable, tamper-evident audit trails record every read, write, and export event on clinical charts.

3. EasyWorkFlow™ Cryptographic Trust Architecture

EasyWorkFlow™ adheres strictly to:

  • United States ESIGN & UETA Acts: Legally binding e-signatures recognized across all 50 states.
  • European Union eIDAS Regulation: Advanced Electronic Signatures (AES) with signer identity linkage and SHA-256 tamper evidence.
  • RFC 3161 Atomic Clock Timestamping: Direct integration with accredited Timestamp Authorities (TSA).

4. EasySchoolFlow™ Student Record Privacy (FERPA)

EasySchoolFlow™ complies with the Family Educational Rights and Privacy Act (FERPA), ensuring educational institutions maintain 100% data ownership with strict role-based access control (RBAC) preventing unauthorized disclosures of student academic performance.
