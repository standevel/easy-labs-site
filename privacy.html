Legal & Regulatory Compliance

Privacy Policy & Data Protection Framework

Effective Date: August 17, 2026 • Version 3.4 • Applicable Globally

Easy Labs Ltd ("Easy Labs", "we", "our", or "us") is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy, confidentiality, and data sovereignty of our enterprise clients, partners, and platform end-users across all software products and consulting engagements.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the product or service you utilize, Easy Labs collects and processes:

  • Account & Consultation Information: Full name, professional email address, organization name, telephone number, and architectural requirements provided through our consultation forms.
  • Product Telemetry: Log metrics, API request counts, latency statistics, and error traces necessary to maintain our 99.99% High Availability SLA.
  • EasyWorkFlow™ Execution Metadata: Signer IP addresses, device user-agents, cryptographic SHA-256 digests, and RFC 3161 timestamps required for legal audit certificate generation.
  • Enterprise Customer Data: Student records in EasySchoolFlow™, financial general ledger records in EsERP™, and Electronic Health Records in CareflowHMS™. All customer data is owned strictly by the enterprise tenant.

2. Legal Bases for Processing (GDPR & UK-GDPR)

We process personal data under the following lawful bases:

  • Contractual Necessity: To provision licensed instances, process payments, and execute Master Services Agreements.
  • Legitimate Interests: To prevent fraud, detect malicious intrusions, and optimize system concurrency.
  • Legal Compliance: To satisfy tax records, healthcare retention regulations, and statutory audit obligations.

3. Data Encryption & Storage Architecture

Encryption in Transit: All HTTP and WebSocket connections are strictly enforced with TLS 1.3 with Perfect Forward Secrecy.
Encryption at Rest: AES-256-GCM envelope encryption is applied across all database clusters, block storage volumes, and backups.
Multi-Tenant Isolation: Logical and schema-level separation ensures zero cross-tenant data leakage. Dedicated single-tenant VPC options are available.
